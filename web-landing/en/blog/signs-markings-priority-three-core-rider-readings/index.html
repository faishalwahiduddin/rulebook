Signs, Markings, Priority: Three Core Rider Readings

By Faishal · 2026-10-04 · 4 min read

Most daily rider reading needs are covered by three groups: signs, markings, and right of way. Signs give orders and bans, markings govern lanes, priority decides who goes first. Read all three in order, star favorites, and check monthly revisions.

Contents
  1. Signs: orders and bans
  2. Markings: lanes and limits

Signs: orders and bans

Signs give orders and bans that must be obeyed on the road. Learn shapes and colors so meaning lands in one glance without stopping to read.

Reading a sign in two seconds

Train yourself to read a sign's meaning in two seconds: shape first, color, then symbol. Sign-reading speed decides smooth on-road decisions. [STAT-NEEDED: most-searched signs] [CITE-NEEDED: internal search-frequency data]

Markings: lanes and limits

Markings govern lanes and limits: divider lines, edges, and special areas. Correct vehicle position follows markings, not the vehicle ahead.

Priority: who goes first at junctions

Right of way decides who goes first at junctions and merges. Learn the order before departure so maneuvers never rely on guesses.

  • Learn order and ban signs.
  • Grasp lane and limit markings.
  • Memorize junction priority order.
  • Star favorite readings for flash review.
  • Check each reading's revision date.

Quick drill before departure

Flash-review the three groups before departure: one sign, one marking, one priority rule. Brief repetition keeps memory fresh.
