A one-screen buku saku checklist

By Faishal Din · 2026-10-19 · 2 min read

When it matters, buku saku lives in three places, and the first one you open is already stale. RuleBook keeps buku saku on-device, so it stays readable with no data plan and no account.

Contents
  1. The smallest version
  2. When to run it

The smallest version

Keep one card with contacts, location, and key numbers that opens without a signal.

When to run it

Refresh the card monthly, or as soon as a number or address changes.

The weekly check

Once a week, look at the part that breaks most often and shrink the target instead of adding willpower.

FAQ

Can I open buku saku offline?

Yes. RuleBook keeps everything on-device, so no data plan is needed.

Do I need an account first?

No. Start without login; export a backup from time to time.

Where do I start?

With the shortest piece. Add scope only after two steady weeks.

What if I miss a few days?

Do not wipe history. Resume from the last entry and note one blocker.
