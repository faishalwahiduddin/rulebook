**Title** (25/30)

> RuleBook: Buku Saku Hukum

**Short description** (79/80)

> Aturan tilang, hak kerja, UU ITE, dan konsumen dalam bahasa awam. Bisa offline.

**Full description** (2086/4000)

<details><summary>show</summary>

RuleBook adalah buku saku hukum Indonesia untuk orang awam. Saat kena razia, ragu soal pesangon, atau curiga ditipu belanja online, kamu bisa langsung cari pasal dan sanksinya, tanpa internet.

Bahasa UU dan PP itu berat. RuleBook meringkasnya jadi boleh, dilarang, dan sanksi, tanpa membuang rujukan pasalnya.

Isi RuleBook:
• 31 butir aturan dalam 6 kategori: lalu lintas, ketenagakerjaan, data pribadi & UU ITE, perlindungan konsumen, K3, dan etika
• Tiap butir berisi ringkasan, dasar hukum, sanksi atau hak, plus daftar yang perlu dilakukan dan dihindari
• Contoh: SIM & STNK, helm SNI, main HP saat berkendara, lembur, cuti tahunan, THR, PHK & pesangon, PKWT, BPJS Ketenagakerjaan, hak subjek data (UU PDP), doxxing, penipuan online, barang cacat, klausula baku
• Pencarian instan berdasarkan judul, kata kunci, atau nomor pasal
• Kalkulator: upah lembur, pesangon PHK (PP 35/2021), THR prorata, simulasi denda tilang, dan sanksi UU ITE
• 5 SOP langkah demi langkah: razia dan tilang, PHK tanpa pesangon, komplain belanja online, kebocoran data 72 jam, dan kecelakaan kerja
• 4 audit kepatuhan dengan persentase progres: kelengkapan berkendara, hak normatif pekerja, K3 tempat kerja, dan keamanan data pribadi
• Simpan pasal favorit dan tulis catatan pribadi
• Bagikan aturan sebagai kartu gambar atau salin ringkasannya
• Cadangkan simpanan dan catatan ke berkas, lalu pulihkan kapan saja
• Tema terang/gelap dan 8 bahasa, termasuk Jawa dan Sunda

Kenapa RuleBook?
Aplikasi hukum lain biasanya cuma berisi teks KUHP atau UU utuh yang panjang. RuleBook fokus ke aturan yang sering kamu hadapi sehari-hari, lengkap dengan kalkulator dan langkah praktis. Semua konten tertanam di aplikasi, jadi bisa dibaca offline. Tanpa akun, dan pencarian serta catatanmu tidak pernah dikirim ke server.

Catatan: RuleBook adalah edukasi hukum praktis, bukan nasihat hukum. Konten diperbarui lewat versi aplikasi baru, jadi untuk kasus nyata tetap cek peraturan terbaru atau konsultasi dengan ahli hukum.

Simpan RuleBook di HP sebelum razia berikutnya.

Bagian dari keluarga aplikasi faishal.id.

**Title** (30/30)

> RuleBook: Indonesian Law Guide

**Short description** (76/80)

> Pocket guide to Indonesian law: traffic, labor, privacy and consumer rights.

**Full description** (2197/4000)

<details><summary>show</summary>

RuleBook is a pocket guide to Indonesian law for everyday people. Stopped at a traffic check, unsure about severance pay, or scammed shopping online? Look up the article and the penalty in seconds, without internet.

This app covers Indonesian law only. It's useful if you live, work or drive in Indonesia, including expats and HR teams working with Indonesian staff.

Legal text is hard to read. RuleBook boils each rule down to what's allowed, what's not, and the sanction, and keeps the legal reference.

What's inside:
• 31 rules in 6 categories: traffic, labor, data privacy and the ITE (cyber) law, consumer protection, workplace safety, and ethics
• Each rule has a summary, legal basis, sanction or right, plus do and don't lists
• Topics like driver's license and vehicle papers, SNI helmets, phone use while driving, overtime, annual leave, religious holiday bonus (THR), layoffs and severance, fixed-term contracts, social security, personal data rights, doxxing, online fraud, defective goods and unfair contract clauses
• Instant search by title, keyword or article number
• Calculators: overtime pay, severance (PP 35/2021), prorated THR, traffic fine estimate and ITE sanctions
• 5 step-by-step SOPs: traffic stops, layoffs without severance, online shopping complaints, 72-hour data breach response, and workplace accidents
• 4 compliance checklists with progress: vehicle papers, worker rights, workplace safety and personal data security
• Bookmark rules and add private notes
• Share a rule as an image card or copy the summary
• Back up bookmarks and notes to a file and restore them anytime
• Light/dark theme and 8 languages

Why RuleBook?
Most law apps just dump the full text of a statute. RuleBook focuses on the rules people actually run into, with calculators and practical steps. Everything is built into the app, so it works offline. No account, and your searches and notes never leave your phone.

Note: RuleBook is practical legal education, not legal advice. Content is updated with new app versions, so for a real case, check the latest regulation or talk to a lawyer.

Keep RuleBook on your phone for the next time you need it.

Part of the faishal.id app family.
